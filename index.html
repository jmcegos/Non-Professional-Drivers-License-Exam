How it works

  • Enter your details, then press Start — the timer begins immediately.
  • Answer one question per screen. Press Submit Answer to lock it in and move on.
  • Submitted answers are final; you cannot return to a previous question.
  • At 00:00 the paper is submitted automatically and blanks are marked incorrect.
  • Afterwards you get your score, a pass/fail verdict, and a full item-by-item review.

What it covers

  • Road signs, signals, and pavement markings
  • Right of way, speed limits, overtaking, and parking
  • Defensive driving and vehicle emergencies
  • RA 8750, RA 10054, RA 10586, RA 10913, and RA 11229
